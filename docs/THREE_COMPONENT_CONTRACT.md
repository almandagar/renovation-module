## v3.6 verification correction — 12 November 2026

The existing files are corrected in place at the user's request; no new supersession is required. This section refines the original v3.6 checklist semantics.

- `physical_resolution` counts resolved and unresolved quantities independently of assignment evidence. `assignments` records EXPLICIT_LIBRARY_ID or FALLBACK_RULE, declared evidence class (default UNSPECIFIED for direct IDs), linked family and available profile-scope fields. A library ID does not imply observed identification. The legacy coverage categories remain for compatibility; UNCLASSIFIED_PHYSICAL means unspecified assignment provenance, not failed physical resolution.
- Both direct IDs and fallback selections check the physical profile's declared country, use, period and place scope. Undeclared scope is reported, not invented. Human compatibility review remains necessary.
- `execution_status`, `integration_completeness`, `physical_coverage_complete` and `empirical_qualification` are separate. Successful preliminary runs remain permitted. Completeness is relative to `integration_scope`: DONOR_PHYSICS, STOCK_PHYSICS or RESOURCE_DELIVERY. All require completed execution, declared source-scope review and an uncertainty design; stock/delivery require independent stock controls; delivery additionally requires capacity and no reported unknown inventory. A visible unresolved remainder need not prevent accounting completeness but prevents full physical coverage.
- `source_scope_manifest` declares registry_ref, registry_version, country and families. Each family declares family_id, source_ref, date, population_unit, geography and cohort_scope. Matching `archetype_evidence_review` records must repeat those scope fields and supply source_ref, decision (USED, EXCLUDED, PROXY_ONLY or UNRESOLVED) and reason. Every calculated cell's `archetype_assignment.family_id` must link to a USED family; fallback rules can supply family_id. The manifest must be grounded in the applicable shared/country registry, including stock owners and fallback families, rather than reconstructed from selected donors alone. Machine completeness is only against this declared scope, not proof of exhaustive research or scientific validity.
- Uncertainty requires source_ref, central_basis and alternatives or an explicit not_applicable_reason for a controlled fixed-input comparison. Declared alternatives require `alternative_run_evidence` for completeness; its presence alone is not independent validation. Retain each alternative's exact inputs, outputs and run hashes for review.
- `aggregate_population_branches` sums already calculated, disjoint branch heat and conserves buildings, dwellings and conditioned area. Unresolved branches retain their quantities and unknown heat; no renormalisation is permitted. Per-area heat uses area weighting. Historical flat weights cannot directly weight kWh/m² unless appropriate area conversion is supplied. Competing whole-stock hypotheses remain separate runs and must not be added as disjoint population branches.

## v3.6 — Mandatory archetype intake and evidence of completion

This section governs research admission where older paragraphs or workbook status labels imply a blanket stop. It does not waive required physical inputs or promote any national forecast. v3.5 essential-construction protection and resource-release accounting are retained.

### Contract

1. Review applicable existing shared and country archetypes, physical donors, historical counts and fallbacks before creating replacements. Record source, date/scope, decision (USED, EXCLUDED, PROXY_ONLY) and reason in `archetype_evidence_review`. A catalogue or mapping alone is not completed integration.
2. Prefer specific compatible evidence; then historically constrained families or conditional mixtures; then compatible period/form physical fallbacks. Use explicit disjoint allocations and a visible unresolved remainder. Lack of measured contemporary subtype shares is not grounds to discard historical or bounded evidence. Do not assign unsupported national percentages.
3. Keep buildings, dwellings and conditioned floor area separate. Supply independent `stock_controls` with opening date, source and all three totals. Historical survival bounds require compatible units/geography and a loss ceiling covering the entire interval. Mixture weights must retain their source population and unit. Flat weights cannot silently become building weights. City refinement replaces parent quantities, never adds stock.
4. Separate original assembly, current component state, operating state and heating equipment. Compute distinct physical alternatives before aggregating outputs. Do not average incompatible wall thicknesses/materials into an invented assembly. Preserve cold attic, heated slope, cellar, ground and party boundaries.
5. Resolve physical inputs through the actual library and run the shared pipeline. Missing measured heat or complete history does not prevent source-labelled conditional physics. Missing required geometry/U/service fields blocks the affected calculation until a justified physical fallback is supplied. Unknown material/task coefficients remain unknown; they restrict resource/delivery claims, not valid baseline heating calculations.
6. Energy-only fallbacks such as POSTWAR_A 189/120/62 remain separate aggregate diagnostics for the unresolved remainder. They cannot supply U-values, quantities or hours to the component solver. Report their population and contribution separately; do not add them to already resolved cohorts.
7. Supply a defensible central scenario and explicit alternatives for unidentified joints. `archetype_uncertainty` records `source_ref`, `central_basis`, `alternatives`; recording alternatives is not evidence of executing them. Retain infeasible combinations and conservation failures visibly as exclusions with reasons.
8. Freeze prediction inputs before using observations. Compare aggregate, bounded-archetype and richer local approaches on matched boundaries; use a few known buildings to test neighbourhood mechanisms and independent aggregates for city stock. Identify renovation works and operating conditions where possible. Report residuals, bias and uncertainty; there is no universal ±10% acceptance threshold. Never score calibration cases as fresh validation.
9. Completion requires resolved inputs, output files, hashes, source review and the machine-generated checklist. Coverage is quantified in buildings, dwellings and conditioned m² for SPECIFIC, PHYSICAL_FALLBACK, UNCLASSIFIED_PHYSICAL and UNRESOLVED. Passing software checks does not establish empirical accuracy or permit authoritative publication.
10. Respect existing ownership. Essential Housing supplies quantities/purposes; the engine accounts for supplied capacity regions and construction work. No location optimiser or internal migration model is introduced. Protect essential/committed work and scale only eligible discretionary construction under the v3.5 contract.

## v3.5 — Essential Housing protection, dated release and PI-headroom counterfactual (12 November 2026)

The PAS `pas_newbuild_dwellings` trajectory remains a **completion-year dwelling target by capacity region**. It does not identify the place or rank of renovations. In addition to the existing immutable `committed: true` flag, forecast `newbuild` events may now carry a source-qualified **`construction_purpose`**:

- `DISCRETIONARY_ADDITIONAL_RES`: uncommitted additional dwellings eligible for proportional PAS reduction.
- `ESSENTIAL_ADDITIONAL_RES`: additional essential dwellings that must survive; requires `essential_source_ref`.
- `SAFETY_REPLACEMENT`: gross replacement dwellings required for documented loss/damage; requires `replacement_source_ref`.
- `ESSENTIAL_NONRES`: hospitals, schools, municipal facilities or other necessary *nonresidential* new buildings; requires `essential_source_ref` and **`dwellings: 0`**. Residential and nonresidential functions in the same event must be separated before release.

Historical and committed construction remain immutable. Legacy unlabelled positive-dwelling newbuilds retain the prior discretionary scaling behaviour. Unlabelled **zero-dwelling** newbuild is safeguarded as `UNCLASSIFIED_NONRES` (it is *not* inferred to be essential); zero-dwelling forecast events no longer require a dummy dwelling target. Source-locked essential and replacement dwellings set a **minimum** on the group’s PAS target. A target below the protected level fails. Newbuilding area, building counts and design-derived capacity reservations are preserved for the protected event. If PAS requires additional essential buildings absent from PI, the construction owner must provide source-qualified physical events and an explicitly feasible capacity; reducing the PI dwelling target alone does not invent new designs.

**Temporal causality fix.** Cancellation does **not** release full-year resources at 1 January. Every curtailed newbuilding event reports its actual original **start date**, affected named task-hours/installed-material requirements, and cancellation identity in `pas_newbuild_reallocation.avoided_construction_starts`. Its removed resource booking is held against the annual capacity **until that exact start date**, for ordinary work as well as PAS extras; `pas_extra` checkpoints cannot precede that date. The first cancellation date per region is a scheduling gate, but resources from subsequent cancellations remain reserved until *their* original starts. Annual trade/material caps remain annual and cannot independently prove monthly or daily labour availability; multiyear physical work still requires explicit stages.

**Like-for-like attribution fix.** For each active PAS reduction, the engine internally reruns the **same PAS work programme** and institutional timing with unchanged eligible renovation/conversion/airtightness requests, weather, stock, priorities, caps and full uncut PI newbuild. This comparator may initiate extra work at the earliest legitimate readiness date even before a later PI newbuild start, but the unchanged PI newbuild reservation protects its resource budget. Thus it can use headroom that already existed under PI without inventing early releases. The output includes both PAS and PI-counterfactual extra-work start dates. `pas_newbuild_reallocation.pi_headroom_comparison` gives per-request PAS admitted m², PI-headroom-admitted m², signed difference, and **positive incremental m²**. Only the latter is a bounded *candidate* increase attributable to reduced construction, conditional on the counterfactual; gross `PAS_EXTRA` work is **not** automatically credited. If the uncut PI pipeline is infeasible under declared construction capacity or readiness, the comparison reports `UNQUALIFIED_PI_COUNTERFACTUAL` with `total_net_incremental_area_m2: null` rather than falsely assigning the extra work to the construction shift. `NO_AVOIDED_NEWBUILD` is explicit. An admitted work area is not automatically an energy saving or verified job-hour transfer.

**Nonresidential works.** `construction_flow.prepare` now permits `dwellings: 0` while requiring positive conditioned area and buildings. Explicit `ESSENTIAL_NONRES` projects must have a real physical design and their own task-hour/material inventory; resource reservations are inside the shared cap, not a dwelling count. This change does not add a generic civil-infrastructure solver: water/sewer/paths belong to the separate construction and infrastructure event/resource owners. Conversion and renovation selection algorithms, archetype physics, ecological place screening, social diffusion and stock-conservation equations are unchanged.

**Software verification and provenance.** `test_pas_newbuild_reallocation.py` has five retained/coherent tests including one corrected test-cap fixture: original PI must be feasible if used as a valid matched comparator; the formerly supplied 15,000 kg cap for 20,000 kg original planned concrete was not. `test_pas_essential_timing_v35.py` adds eight regressions for November-versus-February, protected nonres building, safety replacement, zero-dwelling legacy safeguarding, mandatory purpose source, unscaled trajectory, PI-headroom not credited, and an unqualified over-capacity PI comparator. **13/13 focused tests pass locally.** This is synthetic software QA only; no country data are calibrated, no external schema names changed (`building-stock/2`, `cohort-result/2`), and `runtime_promoted: false` is preserved. The manifest is `3.5-essential-construction-dated-release`. The historic v3.4 physics and scientific validation remain as documented below.

## v3.3 — signed adjacent-zone temperature option (30 October 2026)

Missing IDs, missing periods, unsourced temperatures, shared-weather insertion and legacy-ratio-only geometry fail closed. The quasi-steady balance sums direct `U × surface_area × (inside_temperature − adjacent_temperature)` and then clips net whole-zone heating at zero. Temperature differences can have either sign; a staircase colder than outdoor air is admissible. Direct zone temperatures replace equivalent boundary-factor transmission, not add to it. A direct-temperature calculation has no unique scalar outdoor-equivalent HTC; `heat()` returns `h_w_k: null`, plus `adjacent_zone_mode` and signed interzone exchange. Ventilation retains the existing, separately governed legacy or qualified balanced-flow behaviour.

## v3.2 surface, conditioned-area, transformation and leakage extension (29 October 2026)

This section supersedes v3.1 statements that implicitly make all envelope surfaces exterior, infer conditioned area from built-up area, or treat one component U-state as necessarily uniform across every façade. The input schema remains `building-stock/2`; existing cells may continue using source-tagged archetype ratios. The historical stock and pathways are unchanged.

**Conditioned-area bridge.** `cell.area_m2` remains the explicitly chosen conditioned floor area. `surface_state.floor_area_alternatives` accepts separate sourced level records (`built_up`, `internal`, `usable`, `conditioned`) and returns declared conditioned-fraction bounds or `UNKNOWN_NO_CONVERSION`. It never silently substitutes built-up/usable area for conditioned m², converts a nominal loft or 1986 annex to heated area, or fills a missing central estimate. Floor-area and volume/roof/ceiling boundaries remain distinct.

**Explicit inventory, where evidence or bounded assumptions permit.** Each `surface_inventory` entry is a distinct physical area with `id`, `component`, `area_m2`, `area_basis`, `adjacent`, `source_ref`, optional `orientation_deg`, `boundary_factor`, and a cell-local state in `surface_states`. Set `surface_inventory_scope: complete_thermal_boundary` and leave `geometry_override` unset. `adjacent` is one of `exterior`, `ground`, `unheated_enclosure`, `heated_neighbour`. For heated neighbours transmission to outdoors is zero; ground and unheated-adjacency factors require sourced/prior approximations; exterior factor is one. This is a bounded thermal-screen approximation, NOT a multizone or ground solver. The same areas are used for UA, installed/removed material amounts, task hours and marginal recipient-priority heat-transfer benefit. Legacy component ratios remain marked as ratio-derived; no facade is excluded a second time merely because its source ratio already embeds exposure.

**Dated physical events.** `conversion.physical_change` carries `source_ref`, optional `surface_changes` (`add`, `replace`, `remove` with explicit surface records), optional `surface_states`, and optional `conditioned_area_delta_m2`. Its completion updates the reserved disjoint stock cell; physical history retains event ID and date and reconciles conditioned floor area in the same stock conservation ledger. If also declaring `quantity_delta.area_m2`, do not duplicate the same conditioned-area addition. Expansion and work scope remain distinct from useful-heat renovation savings; missing structural material and labour BOMs must stay visible unknowns. `renovation` can separately target `surface_targets` by surface ID, retaining already-improved façade sections. The first-deep calculation remains a fixed-service reference test and is not an observed first-completion count without dated historical evidence.

**Leakage versus air movement.** Dated `airtightness` events record affected parts/junctions, achieved *measured* or *assumed* pressure-test bounds, measurement/test boundary and event provenance. An `engineering_target` cannot be recorded as achieved. Later measurements replace priors rather than stack heat losses. Pressure-test units qE50 ↔ n50 convert only with compatible declared envelope area and internal volume; these values are NOT operating ACH. Where `operating_airflow_by_period` supplies qualified balanced-flow records with resolved infiltration, window airing, intentional supply, actual heat-recovery scope and operating/fan configuration, that calculated heat-loss coefficient **replaces** the legacy ventilation term. Unqualified natural or extract-only pressure/leakage coupling fails closed; v3.4 adds the explicitly bounded pressure route described above. Fan electricity is exported separately from space-heating carrier consumption and needs downstream end-use reconciliation. The city-v2 local bridge accepts resolved constant ventilation, but refuses changing-by-period ventilation it cannot represent.

**Evidence and verification.** Present-day component observations cannot establish an earlier opening component state without historical evidence. Apparent façade age cannot independently validate a construction-system class derived from the same façade. Derived measured HTC with ventilation adjustment is not fully independent of an airflow prediction sharing those inputs. Separate geometry, system, condition, airflow and solver contributions in held-out tests; do not fit coefficients to a declared holdout. The current Orava/attached-courtyard scenarios are source-semantics and synthetic geometry checks; SMETER's source HTC benchmark is NOT a new resolver error metric. No automatic national calibration, EN/PF/TT promotion or change to PAS/HDE finance exclusions follows.

## Renovation and delivery

A renovation event names a `package_id`, `selector`, requested `area_m2`, start/completion dates and optional explicit priority. Selectors can use a stable `root_id` across successive splits and interventions. Packages specify component targets and optionally heating equipment. Only components still needing improvement generate work, unless explicit renewal is requested. Untreated area remains available; treated cohorts remain eligible for other measures.

Conversion/reoccupation events use explicit sourced quantity deltas and service updates. Reoccupying existing space does not add a new building; its increased heating service remains visible. Complete physical conversion work needs its own package/inventory rather than an unexplained efficiency reduction.

Completion changes the component state. A stable original-state technical heat reference at 20°C and baseline-year weather identifies the first cumulative reduction exceeding 60%; subsequent stages do not create another first deep renovation. This is a **useful-space-heat model definition**, not automatic legal deep-renovation qualification. Heating-system changes affect final energy separately. The baseline comparison includes inherited/history component improvements; no history of work is fabricated for an inferred opening state.

Requests reserve known workforce tasks and installed-material quantities against `capacity[region][year]`. Priority orders competing requests. Capacity can partly fulfil a request; unmet area is exported. Unknown intensity/capacity is explicitly untested, not zero. Current reservations charge the start year: use explicit stages for multiyear work. Legacy bundles require a later programme event for deferred work. The active PAS integration below can retry eligible requests automatically.

### PAS newbuild trajectory and use of released capacity

In the existing `building-stock/2` bundle, set `"pas_newbuild_dwellings":{"district":{"2030":50}}` to set PAS's forecast newbuild dwellings by capacity region and **completion** year. Include every forecast newbuild region/year in the PAS schedule; a zero is a deliberate cancellation. The same bundle field is used in all PAS strategies and timings, with no change to PI or HDE. The existing PAS design mix is scaled proportionally by dwellings, buildings and conditioned area. Historical starts and explicit forecast `"committed":true` events are protected. A trajectory outside the retained/planned range or which cannot fit known capacity is rejected rather than delivered as fewer new homes than requested. Dated design standards still apply.

The scheduler reserves retained newbuild work by **start** year before other requests can spend it. Removed work drops out of stock and resource accounts; capacity is never increased by adding the avoided demand back to the caps. In a region from its first reduction year onward, each PAS strategy can use the remaining known task and installed-material capacity after ordinary starts in that year. It draws only from that strategy's existing renovation, conversion and airtightness requests and respects recipient selectors, priority, dated diffusion, readiness and work duration. Current/overdue work gets first use before bringing future work forward. Capacity is evaluated afresh each year; unused capacity is not banked.

An existing request's area is its default eligible ceiling; optional `pas_eligible_area_m2` can expose a larger known programme scope without creating another event. Optional `pas_ready_from` permits starting future requests early, bounded by diffusion and the forecast horizon; without it, starts are no earlier than the original schedule. Unserved eligible area is retried in following years under the original request identity; planned and extra starts share one ceiling, and a completed cohort slice is not repeated for the same request. Do not provide overlapping duplicate request IDs/scopes for the same work. Physical conversion deltas remain proportional to the original request's work area.

Automatic extra starts require known, compatible capacity for all required named delivery resources and a positive work inventory; they do not assume trades are interchangeable. Deployable capacity after a trade or location transfer belongs in the dated capacity inputs. Results expose `pas_newbuild_reallocation` (trajectory, avoided planned requirements, unused capacity and remaining queue), and the normal stock, energy, materials and task-hour outputs include the work actually scheduled. The existing strategy selector is unchanged. This feature is invoked by the normal shared runtime whenever the trajectory is supplied; no national trajectories or calibrated coefficients are inferred by the code.

Early/central/late dates are alternative diffusion anchors of the same physical event. Required institutional/technical capabilities gate commitment. Qualified donor effects act only from their operational date and in the recipient scope; an unqualified donor does not add capability. These input qualifications still need evidence and must not be treated as research certification. No finance gate is applied to PAS/HDE renovation priority projects.

Pathways share the historical stock. PI inheritance for HDE is represented by retaining inherited components/programmes, not setting fabric work to zero. PAS priorities, Right-to-Housing retention and replacement restrictions belong in explicit pathway programme inputs; this release does not invent national rates for them. Demolition of heritage-marked stock or post-2010 cohorts is protected in the forecast unless a sourced risk exception is supplied. Historical observed demolition remains recordable. Age alone does not confer statutory heritage protection.

## Separate construction and standards

New construction is represented from 2011. Events provide country, owner, per-building useful floor area, total quantities, start/completion dates, legally relevant dates, design or ordered `design_candidates`, and source. Completed new buildings become identifiable stock cohorts. Stock before completion is unaffected, including for construction extending beyond the forecast horizon.

Rules carry jurisdiction, date basis, validity interval, scope, thresholds and source. Date basis may be permit/application, design or completion. National transitional exemptions require a source in the event. Known failing designs are rejected; supplied candidates can select a better design. Missing compliance evidence is UNASSESSED. Observed historical noncompliance can be recorded with a source rather than erased. Declared regulatory metrics are distinct from predicted actual energy. Where a design supplies `standard_weather`, a component-physics heat metric is derived for comparison against a rule using `model_useful_heat_kwh_m2_a`.

The accompanying standards seed records the EU ZEB and WLC schedules and a **partial** voluntary Passivhaus heating/airtightness screen. It is not the national 2011–2035 regulation database. Completion date is an explicit seed interpretation; country-specific application/permit transitional treatment must be checked. The EU WLC size threshold is per building, not the aggregate cohort area. Passivhaus uses its defined treated floor area and complete dated PHI criteria; an annual average under 15 kWh/m² conditioned area is not certification.

WLC has separate A1–A3, A4–A5, B1/B2/B3/B5, B4, B6, B7, C1–C4 and D accounts with assessment period and method reference. A1–A3 can be calculated from supplied product masses and emission factors. Missing modules leave the total unknown. Other modules require qualified lifecycle scenarios, including future energy factors. Disclosure obligations alone do not reduce material or energy use. National numerical WLC limits, as supplied, screen the relevant design metrics; there is no invented uniform EU cap.

## Energy and actual local tests

Useful space heat is evaluated separately for each cohort from its components, exposed geometry, weather periods, service temperature, ventilation, bridges and usable gains. Gas/wood/electricity/DH conversion uses explicit equipment factors; HP electricity and ambient heat reconcile. A supplied PM emission factor gives conditional emissions, not ambient particulate concentration. Atmospheric dispersion, other emitters and stove operation prevent PM concentration from uniquely identifying renovation prevalence.

Annual energy is split into pre-2011 and new-since-2011 stock and final carriers. Within-year events currently prorate annual-weather exposure by days: this is documented approximation, not dynamic thermal simulation. This engine's heat output is SPACE HEATING only; DHW, cooling, appliances and network boundary reconciliation remain in the shared accounting/validation interfaces.

`building_neighbourhood_adapter.export_case` takes exact local cell IDs, consistent population/area/period boundaries and dated weather. It exports a frozen prediction and heterogeneous zones to `city-physical-tests/2`. Actual register buildings or disjoint neighbourhood cohorts can be used; no national median fallback exists. Local observations/hypotheses remain external, including incomplete temperature and carrier evidence. Geometry overrides and local sources are traceable. A synthetic identity fixture tests the interface; it is not a measurement of an actual building. [Current parallel city module](export-reference:296c377ae5449ac0) and its Paris evidence remain unchanged.

## Outputs and verification

`cohort_results.json`: dated stock snapshots, annual heating by stock origin/carrier, component event ledger, resource commitments, unmet demand, construction standards and WLC. `material_inventory.json`: completed physical interventions. `resolved_country_inputs.json`: exact run inputs. `run_manifest.json`: hashes, status and limitations. Optional supplied `stock_validation` routes to the existing shared diagnostic engine; absent observations do not become successful empirical validation.

Central-timing strategies are ranked by final-year final space-heating energy for each pathway; the best is default and alternatives appear in Details. This is a conditional ranking; adequacy requires the independent comfort evidence. The software fixture exercises nine alternatives, not nine calibrated country strategies.

40 acceptance tests pass, including the parallel city-v2 interface. Tests cover state conservation, repeated stages, no duplicated measures, history dates, materials/workforce constraints, heritage/post-2010 survival, standards-date selection, alternative construction designs, WLC separation, donor qualification, marginal inference and local geometry. Old aggregate-engine test counts are not represented as validation of this replacement.
